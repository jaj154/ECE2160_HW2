=== Critter Power Experiment Harness (v3: refined methodology) ===
Workload: full pipeline (io->memory->compute), 4096 ticks/call, looped ~3.0 s
per repeat, 5 repeats per config. Settle 400 ms + warm-up pass before timing.
Frequency sampled DURING load. Identical synthetic data every call (fixed seed).
Captured baseline config: cpu0 governor=ondemand, 4 cpu(s) present.
