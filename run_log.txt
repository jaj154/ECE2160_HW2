=== Critter Power Experiment Harness (v2: sustained-load sampling) ===
Workload: full pipeline (io_unit -> memory_unit -> compute_unit), 4096 ticks/call,
looped for ~3.0 s per trial so the power monitor gets a real sample window.
Identical synthetic sensor data on every call (fixed RNG seed).
Captured baseline config: cpu0 governor=ondemand, 4 cpu(s) present.
